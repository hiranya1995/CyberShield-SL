🛡️ Visual Breakdown: How to Classify & Protect Citizen Data at Work
1

🟢 Public Level (Freely Shareable)

Information meant for open public release (e.g. published government gazettes, office opening hours, public circulars). Can be placed on noticeboards or websites.

2

🔵 Official / Internal Use Only

Internal workplace communications, staff duty rosters, and meeting minutes. Should stay within government office departments.

3

🟡 Confidential Citizen Data (PDPA 2022)

Citizen NIC copies, land registry deeds, birth/marriage certificates, and Samurdhi/welfare records. Requires strict access control and encrypted storage.

4

🔴 Restricted / Top Secret & Secure Disposal

Highest sensitivity government files and security documents. Physical paper copies must be shredded with cross-cut shredders — never thrown in regular waste bins!

✅ What Government Staff SHOULD Do

  • Always lock your PC screen (Win + L) when stepping away from your desk.
  • Shred physical paper documents containing citizen NICs or personal data.
  • Store citizen files in locked cabinets or official encrypted file systems.

❌ What Government Staff Should NEVER Do

  • Never leave printed citizen files sitting unattended on open office desks or printers.
  • Never email unencrypted lists of citizen NIC numbers to personal Gmail addresses.
  • Never discard citizen registration forms into regular rubbish bins.
4 Government Data Classification Levels

Every document handled at the Divisional Secretariat must be treated according to its sensitivity level:

🟢 Public

Press releases, gazettes, public circulars. No restriction.

🔵 Internal / Official

Internal office memos, staff duty lists. Office staff only.

🟡 Confidential

Citizen NIC records, land deeds, salary slips. Need-to-know basis.

🔴 Restricted / Secret

Security audits, law enforcement files. Strict approval required.

Sri Lanka Personal Data Protection Act (PDPA) No. 9 of 2022

As government officers, we are legally required to protect citizen privacy. The 5 core PDPA principles are:

  • 1. Lawful Purpose: Collect citizen data only for official government work.
  • 2. Data Minimisation: Ask only for essential details needed for the application.
  • 3. Data Accuracy: Ensure citizen records are correct and up-to-date.
  • 4. Storage Limits: Safely destroy personal files once official retention periods end.
  • 5. Incident Reporting: Report any lost files or breaches to your Divisional Secretary immediately.
